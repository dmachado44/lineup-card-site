FAQ

Questions boards always ask

Plain answers, no legalese. If your board has a question that isn't here, email us — we'll answer it for everyone.

Is it really free for every coach in our league?

Yes. Every coach in a chartered Little League or rec league that links to Lineup Card gets full access to the core lineup builder at no cost — no trial, no per-team fee, no "premium" lockouts on the basics. That's the whole offer.

What data do you collect on players?

As little as the coach decides to enter. Lineup Card works with first-name-only rosters; there's no required field for last name, birthdate, or any other identifier. We don't collect parent contact info, addresses, or anything tied to a child. The full details are in our privacy policy.

Do you support continuous batting order and mandatory play?

Yes. Continuous batting order is the default. Defensive positions auto-rotate across innings so playing time is balanced across the season, and mandatory play tracking flags any player who hasn't met the minimum yet so the coach can see it before the lineup is locked.

Will you market other products to our parents?

No. We do not contact league parents directly, and we don't sell or share contact information.

To be specific about what's on offer: we do make a companion iOS app called Lineup DJ — it handles batter walkout music and announcer intros. An individual coach who wants those features can choose to subscribe to Lineup DJ from the App Store, the same way they'd subscribe to anything else. That's a personal decision a coach can make for themselves. Lineup Card itself stays free for every coach in your league.

What if a coach also wants the paid features?

The lineup builder your league cares about — auto-rotation, fair playing time, printable cards, Little League rule support — is free. There are some advanced features (multiple teams, strategy tuning, season history, link sharing) that an individual coach can subscribe to if they want, billed to that coach personally for a couple of dollars a month. The league has no role in that, and it never bills back to the league.

Who built this and why?

Lineup Card is built by Dan Machado, a Little League coach in Southern California, under his independent studio Turn Two Labs. The "why" is in the founder section above: he got tired of doing position math in his Notes app the night before every game, so he built a tool that does it instead. The league-friendly pricing is intentional — leagues that link to Lineup Card make it sustainable, and in return their coaches get the tool free.
