Questions, answered

Is Pro really included with my Leadoff iOS subscription?+
Yes. If you have an active Leadoff subscription on iOS, sign in here with the same account and you get full Pro access at no extra cost. Your roster, players, and position preferences sync automatically.
What do I get with Pro?+
Pro unlocks unlimited teams, the advanced fair-play lineup engine, the strategy slider for competitive play, full game history and team statistics, lineup sharing with coaches and parents, and priority support.
How does the 7-day free trial work?+
Start the trial and you get every Pro feature for 7 days at no charge. If it is not for you, cancel before the trial ends and you will not be billed.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes. Cancel at any time from your account settings. You keep Pro through the end of your current billing period.
Do I need the iOS app to use Leadoff on the web?+
No. Anyone can subscribe to Pro directly on the web. iOS subscribers get it included, but the web lineup builder works great on its own.
